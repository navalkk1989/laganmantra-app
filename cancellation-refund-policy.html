Cancellation & Refund Policy

LaganMantra · Registered office: Building no 246 Bijwasan, Kapashera to Najafgarh Road, South West Delhi, Pincode: 110061
Effective date: 12 October 2026

In short

All payments made on LaganMantra are final and non-refundable. The only exception is a duplicate payment – when you are charged more than once for the same plan. In that case the extra amount is refunded in full.
Saar: LaganMantra par kiya gaya har payment non-refundable hai. Refund sirf tab milega jab ek hi plan ke liye galti se do baar payment kat gaya ho (duplicate payment).

1. What counts as a duplicate payment

A duplicate payment means the same premium plan was paid for more than once for the same LaganMantra account at around the same time – for example because of a network error, a double tap on the pay button, or a bank/UPI glitch – and the money was received by us more than once. Only the extra (duplicate) amount is refunded; the first payment stays and your plan remains active.

2. How to request a refund for a duplicate payment

Contact us within 7 days of the payment by phone/WhatsApp +91 8448103956 or email support@laganmantra.com, or use Contact Us in the app. Please share: your registered mobile number, the plan name, the date of payment and the transaction / payment IDs (UTR or Razorpay ID) of both payments, with screenshots if possible.

3. Verification and timeline

We check the request against our payment records and the payment gateway / bank. Once the duplicate payment is confirmed, the extra amount is refunded to the same payment method (UPI, card, net banking or bank account) within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it in your account.

4. When a refund is NOT given

No refund is given in any other case, including: change of mind; not finding a suitable match; not using the plan or using it only partly; unused days of a plan; choosing the wrong plan; upgrading or downgrading a plan; a member not replying or declining requests; or an account that is blocked, suspended or deleted for breaking our Terms & Conditions.

5. Failed or pending transactions

If money is debited but the payment fails or is not received by us, it is not a refund case – your bank or the payment gateway reverses such amounts automatically, usually within 5–7 working days. If your plan is not activated after a successful payment, contact us and we will activate it.

6. Cancellation

Once a payment is made, the plan cannot be cancelled and the amount cannot be refunded. You may stop using LaganMantra at any time – your plan simply stays active until its expiry date. Deleting your account or not using the plan does not make it cancellable or refundable, and there is no partial (pro-rata) refund. The only refund is for a duplicate payment, as explained in points 1–3 above.
Saar: Payment karne ke baad plan cancel nahi hota aur paisa wapas nahi milta. Refund sirf duplicate payment par milega (upar point 1–3).
