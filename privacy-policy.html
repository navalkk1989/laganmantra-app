Privacy Policy

LaganMantra · Registered office: Building no 246 Bijwasan, Kapashera to Najafgarh Road, South West Delhi, Pincode: 110061
Effective date: 12 October 2026

1. Who we are and scope

The Company is the data fiduciary for personal data processed through the Platform, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

From you: mobile number, email, name, gender, date of birth, location, religion, caste, mother tongue, education, occupation, NRI status, height, weight, complexion, Manglik status, description, photos, bio-data files and partner preferences. Some of this is sensitive (for example religion and caste) and is collected only with your explicit consent for matchmaking. Automatically: device and browser details, IP address, approximate location if you allow it, and usage data. Communications: chats, interests, contact-form messages and chatbot queries. Payments: plan, amount, transaction reference and method; card or bank credentials are handled by the payment provider and not stored by us.

3. Why we use it

To create and show your profile; suggest matches as per your preferences; run interests, notifications and chat; verify your mobile by OTP; keep the Platform safe (spam, fraud, abusive or adult content); process payments and activate plans; give support; meet legal duties; and improve the service.

4. Consent

We process your data on the basis of your consent given when you register and accept these documents, and for legitimate uses permitted by law. You can withdraw consent or delete your account at any time through Contact Us; this may stop the service. If a family member registers for an adult, that person's consent is required.

5. Who can see your information

Your profile is visible to other registered members as per your settings. Photos can be set to show to everyone or only after a request is accepted. Your mobile number and email stay hidden unless you choose to show them. Anything you show to a member can be saved or shared by them; we cannot control this.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with: service providers who work for us under confidentiality (SMS/OTP, hosting, payments, maps, support tools); authorities when required by law; and a buyer or successor in a business transfer, who must honour this policy. We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing without your consent.

7. Automated checks and blocking

We use automated tools and staff review to detect abusive language, sexual chat and adult images. These checks can lead to blocking of an account. If you think this was a mistake, write to the Grievance Officer.

8. Cookies and local storage

The Platform uses browser storage and similar technology to keep you signed in, remember preferences and improve performance. You can clear it from your browser settings, but some features may stop working.

9. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and afterwards only as long as needed for the purposes above, for legal, tax and accounting requirements, to resolve disputes and to prevent fraud. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

10. Security

We use reasonable security practices such as access control and encryption in transit. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your OTP private and log out on shared devices. If a personal data breach occurs we will inform affected users and the authorities as required by law.

11. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, update or erase your data, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and make a grievance. Many details can be changed in Profile > Edit profile. For other requests use Contact Us; we respond within a reasonable time and not later than the period prescribed by law.

12. Children

The Platform is only for persons 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect data of minors and delete such accounts when found.

13. Data outside India

Some service providers may store or process data outside India, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards. NRI members understand their data may be accessed from India.

14. Third-party links

The Platform may link to third-party sites (for example maps or payment pages). Their privacy practices are their own; read their policies.

15. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the new effective date. Continued use means you accept the changes.

16. Grievance Officer and contact

For privacy questions or complaints, contact the Grievance Officer at the registered office shown above, by email or phone shown in Contact Us. We acknowledge within 24 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India as provided under law.
