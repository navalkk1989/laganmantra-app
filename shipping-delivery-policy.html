In short

LaganMantra sells only online (digital) matrimony membership services. Nothing is shipped by post or courier, so there are no shipping charges. Your premium plan is delivered to your LaganMantra account on the website and the Android app.
Saar: Hum sirf online membership dete hain – koi saamaan courier se nahi bheja jaata. Plan aapke LaganMantra account mein chalu hota hai.

1. What is delivered

A premium plan unlocks the services shown on the Plans page for the plan period – for example sending interests, chat, seeing the mobile number of members who accepted your interest, audio / video calls and other premium features. The exact services of each plan are shown before you pay.

2. When is it delivered

Online payment (Razorpay – UPI, cards, net banking, wallets): the plan is activated automatically within a few minutes after the payment is successful.
Direct UPI / bank transfer / QR code: the plan is activated after we verify the payment (UTR / transaction ID or screenshot), usually within 2 hours during working hours (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM), and at the latest within 24 hours.

3. Where is it delivered

On the LaganMantra account linked to your registered mobile number. You can use it on any device by logging in to the website or the Android app. You receive a notification when the plan is active, and the invoice is available in My Profile → My invoices.

4. Plan not active?

If your plan is not active within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us by phone/WhatsApp +91 8448103956 or email support@laganmantra.com, or use Contact Us with your registered mobile number and the payment / transaction ID. We will activate it or, for a duplicate payment, refund it as per the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

5. Cancellation and refunds

Please see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
